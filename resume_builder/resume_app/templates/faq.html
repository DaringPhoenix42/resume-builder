Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions about Space AstroResume. If you don’t find what you’re looking for, feel free to contact us.

How do I create a resume?

Simply go to the Create Resume page, fill out the form, and preview your resume.

Can I download my resume?

Yes, you can download your resume in PDF format after previewing it.

Is Space AstroResume free to use?

Yes, our basic features are completely free. We may offer premium features in the future.

How do I contact support?

Visit the Contact Us page to send us a message.

Can I customize my resume?

Yes, our templates are fully customizable. You can edit the content and layout to suit your needs.

Do you store my data?

No, we do not store any of your personal data. Your resume is generated on the fly and can be downloaded directly to your device.
